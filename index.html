Welcome to Raheja Lunaris

Raheja Lunaris is an astonishing residential tower in the exceptional township - Raheja District in Vashi NX. It comprises 2 & 3 BHK apartments efficiently designed with the finest interiors, contemporary features, and branded CP fittings.

The efficient layout of apartments ensures optimum space utilisation with zero wastage. The township comprises stunning residential, commercial, and retail clusters. Along with the proposed 5-acre District Park, the community offers amenities like a clubhouse, spa, indoor games, swimming pool, kids play area, jogging track, senior citizen corner, gymnasium, banquet hall, and more.

About Raheja Universal

Founded in 1980, Raheja Universal is a leading real estate firm in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). While the company started by developing luxury residential properties in its early years, it has now expanded to affordable residences, IT parks & commercial towers. With its portfolio boasting more than 50 properties, Raheja Universal has won many awards in recent years.
